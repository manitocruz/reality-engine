# Validation Checklist

Use this checklist before approving any transformation.

## Pattern Quality
- Is the input pattern decode explicit?
- Are observed features distinguished from inferred features?
- Is ambiguity acknowledged where needed?
- Is the mapping stronger than mere word association?

## Affordance Logic
- Does the current world actually contain a relevant affordance?
- Is the target specific enough to transform?
- Is the chosen affordance more justified than the alternatives?

## Operator Logic
- Is the operator clearly named?
- Does the operator fit the input-to-world relationship?
- Can the operator’s effect be described concretely?

## Resonance Depth
- Is the `resonance_depth` stated?
- Does the scale of change match that depth?
- Is the transformation too total for the assigned depth?

## Delta Quality
- Is there a clear delta summary?
- Are changed elements listed?
- Are persisted elements listed?
- Are remapped elements listed?
- Are scars or anomalies listed if needed?

## Continuity
- Do Mira and Iven retain memory continuity unless explicitly changed?
- Do Mira and Iven retain bodily continuity unless explicitly changed?
- If embodiment changed, is continuity still legible?
- Are non-protagonist continuity assumptions declared rather than guessed?

## Contradiction Handling
- Does the proposal contradict existing canon?
- If yes, was that contradiction resolved, remapped, or marked as an anomaly?
- Are new scars tracked instead of hand-waved?

## Story Usability
- Could the Storyteller Chat immediately narrate this without inventing missing canon?
- Does the transformation create real consequences instead of just aesthetic drift?
- Does the present state remain readable?

## Final Decision
Approve only if all of the following are true:
- explicit
- legible
- bounded
- trackable
- continuity-safe
- usable as canon
