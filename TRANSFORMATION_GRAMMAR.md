# Transformation Grammar

## Purpose
This document defines how input patterns may validly transform the world.

## Core Formula
Input Pattern + World Affordance + Operator + Resonance Depth + Continuity Handling = Valid Transformation Candidate

## Pattern Categories
The Transformer Chat should look for:
- semantic patterns
- symbolic patterns
- emotional patterns
- structural patterns
- rhythmic patterns
- tonal patterns
- thematic patterns
- archetypal patterns
- relational patterns
- contrastive patterns

## World Affordance
A world affordance is a feature of the current reality that is available to transformation.

Common affordance domains:
- location
- object
- faction
- law
- social role
- ritual
- environment
- memory field
- language regime
- weather logic
- infrastructure
- symbolic anchor

## Operator Set

### Amplify
Increase the intensity, visibility, or influence of a matched pattern.

### Invert
Reverse the valence, direction, or role of a matched pattern.

### Transpose
Move a pattern from one domain to another.

### Echo
Cause a pattern to repeat or manifest in distributed form.

### Bind
Tie two previously separate patterns or systems together.

### Split
Separate one pattern into distinct manifestations.

### Fuse
Merge two patterns into a new hybrid condition.

### Recurse
Cause a pattern to self-apply across levels.

### Reveal
Expose a latent pattern already present in the world.

### Suppress
Reduce or conceal a pattern’s expression without fully removing it.

### Crystallize
Make an abstract or unstable pattern concrete.

### Dissolve
Reduce structure, boundaries, or fixed distinctions.

### Recontextualize
Keep the entity but change its meaning, function, or narrative role.

## Valid Mapping Rules
A mapping is stronger when:
- input and world share a pattern domain
- the transformation has a clear target
- the operator produces legible consequences
- the effect scale fits resonance depth
- continuity remains trackable

A mapping is weaker when:
- the relation is vague
- the target is arbitrary
- the output cannot be expressed as a delta
- the effect scale exceeds support

## Rejection Rules
Reject or revise if:
- no valid affordance exists
- the operator is unjustified
- the effect is too total for the resonance depth
- protagonist continuity is broken without handling
- the change is decorative but presented as systemic
- the transformation erases rather than remaps major world elements without explanation

## Resonance Depth Scale
- 0: cosmetic
- 1: local symbolic
- 2: environmental / situational
- 3: systemic / social
- 4: metaphysical
- 5: ontological rewrite

## Delta Principle
Every approved transformation must be writable as:
- what changed
- what persisted
- what remapped
- what scars were created
- what the protagonists retained
- what new tensions appeared

## Preferred Transformation Style
The best transformations:
- are surprising but legible,
- alter conditions rather than merely describe vibes,
- deepen world consequence,
- create future narrative hooks,
- leave traces of prior states.
