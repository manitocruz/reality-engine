# Protagonists

## Continuity Rule
These protagonists automatically retain memory continuity and bodily continuity across transformations unless a canon delta explicitly changes that.

---

## Protagonist 1

### Name
Mira Vale

### Role
Former junior weather clerk, now unauthorized resonance-sensitive investigator

### Body Continuity
Stable human body continuity

### Memory Continuity
Retains memory across all approved transformations by default

### Core Traits
- precise
- suspicious
- emotionally restrained
- pattern-sensitive
- deeply responsive to language drift

### Anchor Functions
- notices symbolic inconsistencies in civic records
- detects weather-law deviations
- serves as a continuity witness when reality changes

### Current Motivations
- find the source of illegal resonance events
- determine whether the Choir is hiding older transformation incidents
- protect civilians from uncontrolled weather-law shifts

### Current Scars
- hears prior legal phrasing during storm pressure
- experiences involuntary recall when touching etched glass

---

## Protagonist 2

### Name
Iven Thorne

### Role
Harbor runner and former contraband courier with high survival instinct and strong embodied continuity

### Body Continuity
Stable human body continuity

### Memory Continuity
Retains memory across all approved transformations by default

### Core Traits
- adaptable
- sardonic
- loyal under pressure
- physically grounded
- socially perceptive

### Anchor Functions
- tracks practical changes in streets, labor systems, and city behavior
- notices continuity failures in people and institutions
- grounds transformations in bodily consequence and urban reality

### Current Motivations
- keep harbor routes usable
- protect key people in the docks from civic weather manipulation
- understand why certain songs now alter street conditions

### Current Scars
- reflexively navigates routes that no longer fully exist
- carries muscle memory for doors that sometimes appear only in fog

---

## Shared Dynamic
Mira and Iven trust each other under pressure but disagree on method.
Mira seeks pattern truth.
Iven seeks survivable reality.
Their combined perspective helps preserve legibility after transformations.

## Identity Persistence Standard
A protagonist remains the same protagonist if all of the following still hold:
- self-recognition persists
- memory thread remains substantially continuous
- bodily perspective remains trackable
- the delta does not explicitly sever identity continuity
