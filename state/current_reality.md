# Current Reality

## Reality ID
reality_000

## Reality Title
The City of Glass Weather

## Canon Status
ACTIVE

## Present Summary
The world is centered on a coastal city where weather is archived, traded, and enforced through civic ritual. Rain, fog, lightning, drought, and wind are not merely meteorological events but categorized public conditions stored in the Archive of Atmospheres and released by licensed weather clerks under municipal law.

The city is materially real, socially stratified, symbolically unstable, and metaphysically permeable. Language, music, collective emotion, and repeated civic forms can alter the behavior of weather and, in rare cases, alter the boundary between symbolic and material conditions.

## Core World Premise
Meaning can become environment if enough resonance accumulates.

## Active World Layers

### Physical Layer
- Dense coastal city with mirrored towers, tram lines, salt canals, and fog bridges
- Weather-sensitive architecture changes opacity depending on atmospheric pressure and public sentiment
- The harbor is functional but partially restricted by ritual law

### Social Layer
- The Weather Clerks regulate legal release of stored atmospheric conditions
- The Choir of Measures audits emotional and tonal disturbances that might destabilize reality
- The Rag Market trades contraband objects carrying symbolic weather residue
- Most citizens accept civic weather management as normal, though black-market alterations are increasing

### Symbolic Layer
- Glass symbolizes memory, storage, reflection, and fragility
- Rain symbolizes disclosure
- Fog symbolizes ambiguity, mercy, concealment, and delayed consequence
- Lightning symbolizes judgment, revelation, and irreversible decision
- Wind symbolizes rumor, mobility, and contagion of meaning

### Metaphysical Layer
- Repetition increases resonance
- Public ritual stabilizes law-like reality
- Songs, vows, chants, and recurring phrases can produce measurable weather-symbolic effects
- Strong enough resonance can shift legal weather into social reality and social reality into metaphysical law

## Factions
- Weather Clerks: custodians of licensed atmospheric release
- Choir of Measures: auditors of tonal and emotional instability
- Rag Traders: black-market handlers of symbolic residue
- Harbor Union: practical labor force with growing resentment toward ritual weather restrictions

## Active Tensions
- Illegal weather alterations are spreading through poorer districts
- The Choir suspects recent songs are bypassing normal resonance thresholds
- The Harbor Union wants open access to storm-release schedules
- Citizens report hearing prior versions of street names during fog events
- Several legal weather ledgers contain entries in handwriting no clerk recognizes

## Known Transformation Scars
- On damp mornings, some windows briefly reflect an older skyline no longer present
- The word “mercy” causes nearby glass to frost from the inside
- Tram bells occasionally ring in rhythms not used by current city schedules

## Current Weather Regime
Licensed fog in the mornings, controlled clear intervals at noon, rain only by permit after dusk.

## Narrative Tone
Elegant, uncanny, civic, emotionally charged, and slightly brittle.

## Canonical Protagonist Relevance
Two protagonists currently move through this world with full memory continuity and bodily continuity. Their awareness of prior discontinuities is greater than that of the surrounding population.

## Immediate Story Pressure
The city is close to a threshold event in which symbolic weather control may stop being containable by municipal procedure.
